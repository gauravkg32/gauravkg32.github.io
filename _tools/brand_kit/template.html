The mark

The logo is an open nozzle, tilted up as if taking off. Each part carries a piece of the story.

Nitrous

NOZ comes from the nitrous boost in racing games. The brand promise is the same: a boost for your business.

Nozzle

The flame comes straight out of the open end, so the shape reads as thrust, not as a gas cylinder.

Charge

The green fill stops at a slanted line, like a charge meter. Where the letters cross the fill they turn into cut-outs.

Lift-off

The whole mark is tilted up 25°. Moving forward, and climbing.

Logo files

Every version is a vector file (SVG) that stays sharp at any size. PNG copies are made on demand at 2048 px wide, or 1024 px for icons.

Space and size

Give the logo room, and keep it big enough that NOZ stays readable.

Clear space

Keep an empty margin around the logo at least as tall as the nozzle's body (marked x). No text, edges or other logos inside it.

Smallest sizes

Below these sizes the lettering turns to mush. Switch to the icon instead.

  • Favicons, app icons and profile pictures: always the icon.

Don't

The logo only works as drawn. These are the mistakes that come up most.

{{DONTS}}

Colour

Mostly near-black and green. Blue is only for the flame and small highlights; if it shows up everywhere it stops meaning anything. Never use green against blue on its own to tell two things apart.

Status colours, for apps

The NOZ lettering

The letters in the logo are JetBrains Mono ExtraBold Italic, thickened and turned into shapes. Use the logo files. Never retype NOZ in a font to make a logo.

One company name, and plain names for everything inside it.

Company

NOZ Studio

Always in full the first time. "NOZ" on its own only in tight spaces, such as the logo and app icons. NOZ is always in capitals.

What we say

The core story, ready to say out loud or paste into a message.

10 seconds

NOZ Studio builds and runs business software, like order booking, sales tracking and stock, so growing businesses can get off spreadsheets and WhatsApp.

30 seconds

Most growing businesses run on paper, Excel and WhatsApp groups, and things slip. NOZ Studio sets up software for your orders, sales and stock, tailored to how you work, and then we run it: hosting, daily backups, updates and support for one monthly fee. Your team gets an app that works even with no signal, and your data is always yours to export.

NOZ Studio is the managed software studio for growing businesses that run on spreadsheets, paper and WhatsApp, who want their orders, sales and stock in one place without hiring an IT team, because we build the software, host it, back it up and keep improving it for one monthly fee.

Imagery

Show the product, not stock photos.

Pictures

Real screens of real products on near-black. No stock photos of handshakes or people at laptops, and no AI-generated people.

Illustration

Simple drawn mock-ups of dashboards, phones and maps. Thin "boost" lines at the logo's 25° are the one decorative motif.

Icons

Line icons on a 24 px grid, 1.8 px stroke, round ends. Green on a faint green tile, or in the text colour.
